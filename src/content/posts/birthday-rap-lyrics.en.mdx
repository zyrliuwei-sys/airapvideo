**The short answer:** a good birthday rap is short, uses the person's name or one real detail about them, and ends each pair of lines on a punchline. Below are 20 original, clean bars sorted by who the birthday is for. Swap in names and details and they are ready to use in a card, a toast or a caption.

## For a best friend

> Another year older, but you're still the same,
> late to the party but you're still the name.

> We've been friends so long that our jokes need a map,
> happy birthday, legend, now go take a nap.

> You're the one I call when the plan falls through,
> the cake's got candles, but the wish is you.

> From the back of the class to the front of the line,
> happy birthday, partner, you're still doing fine.

## For a sibling

> We fought for the front seat for twenty-odd years,
> today it's all yours, along with these cheers.

> You stole all my clothes and you blamed it on me,
> but happy birthday anyway, see what a saint I can be.

> Same parents, same house, same terrible jokes,
> happy birthday from the better of the folks.

## For a parent

> You taught me to drive and you're still not recovered,
> happy birthday to the best dad, no, the best mother.

> You said "when you're older you'll understand",
> I'm older, I get it, now let's eat the cake, man.

> Every year wiser, every year cooler,
> still the only boss and the only real ruler.

## For a partner

> I'd skip every party to sit with you,
> but it's your party, so I guess I'll come too.

> Out of all of the birthdays, this one's the best,
> because I get to spend it with you, and forget the rest.

> You're the beat in my song and the hook in my verse,
> happy birthday, my love, you're my favorite verse.

## For a coworker

> You fixed the printer, you're a legend for real,
> happy birthday, now let's take a long lunch deal.

> You answer my emails in under a day,
> that's rare in this office, so hip hip hooray.

## About getting older (use with care)

> Another year passed and your knees made a sound,
> the candles cost more than the cake that we found.

> You're not getting older, you're vintage, it's true,
> like a classic old record, scratched, but still new.

> They lit all the candles, the fire alarm cried,
> you're not old, you're just extra bright inside.

## How to make the lyrics feel personal

1. **Add their name.** It instantly turns a generic line into theirs.
2. **Use one real detail.** "Late to the party" only works if they always are.
3. **Keep it short.** Four lines read well on a card; two lines work as a caption.
4. **End warm.** Roast if you like, but finish on something kind.

**The short answer:** you can make a rap video of your dog or cat from a single photo. Upload one photo of your pet and one of yourself (or a second pet), and the AI puts both on one stage, rapping together, in a 10-second vertical video. The trick is the photo: one animal, face visible, roughly looking at the camera.

## Why pet rap videos work

Pets doing human things is one of the most reliable formats on TikTok and Reels. A cat with attitude or a dog that looks like it has opinions is already halfway to a rap verse. Add a beat and a mic and it writes the joke for you.

## Three pairings to try

### 1. You and your pet

The classic. You lead on the left, your pet hypes on the right, or the other way round if your pet is clearly the star. This works especially well for pet birthdays and "gotcha day" anniversaries.

### 2. Two pets

A cat and a dog, two rival cats, or a puppy and the old dog who tolerates it. Since each one comes from its own photo, they do not need to be in the same room, or even the same house.

![A cat and a dog curled up together on the grass](/imgs/blog/pet-together.jpg)

### 3. Your pet and a friend's pet

Two friends, two pets, one duet. Post it on both accounts and tag each other.

## Which pet photos work best

- **One animal per photo.** Crop out other pets and people.
- **Face visible.** The face should be large, sharp and roughly facing the camera.
- **Even light.** Daylight from a window beats flash, which turns eyes into glowing dots.
- **Calm moment.** Action shots are fun, but motion blur makes the face harder to animate.
- **Similar framing.** If the other photo is a person from the waist up, use a pet photo that shows a similar amount of body.

![A boxer dog looking straight at the camera: a great source photo](/imgs/blog/pet-dog.jpg)

Results with animals vary more than with people, especially how the mouth moves. If the first render looks off, the fix is usually a sharper, more front-facing photo.

**The short answer:** to do the AI rap duo trend, turn two photos into a short rap video of two people, download the vertical MP4, and post it with a short caption and TikTok's AI-generated label. With airapvideo the whole thing takes about 10 minutes, including about 3 to 5 minutes of rendering.

## What the trend is

Two people, often friends, a couple, a parent and kid, or a person and a pet, appear on a stage together and rap a short verse. Neither of them filmed anything. The clip is made from two ordinary photos, which is exactly why it is funny: people who would never rap on camera are suddenly doing it.

## Step 1: Pick the duo

The best duos have contrast: the loud friend and the quiet one, a grandparent and a teenager, you and your cat. If you need ideas, see [12 AI rap video ideas](/blog/ai-rap-video-ideas).

## Step 2: Pick the photos

You need one photo of each person:

- **One person per photo,** cropped tight enough that they are the clear subject.
- **Face sharp and visible,** roughly facing the camera.
- **Similar framing** in both photos, for example two waist-up shots.

More detail in [which photos work best](/blog/best-photos-for-ai-rap-video).

## Step 3: Create the video

1. Open the [generator](/#create) in your phone or desktop browser. There is no app to install.
2. Upload both photos. The person on the left leads the verse; the right side hypes.
3. Click **Create**. There are no prompts or settings.
4. Wait about 3 to 5 minutes. Every video is **10 seconds in vertical 9:16**, the native TikTok format.
5. Download the MP4. It has no watermark.

You do not need an account to start. A single video is $9.90; packs of 3 and 5 are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Step 4: Post it on TikTok

1. Upload the MP4 to TikTok.
2. **Turn on the AI-generated content label** in the post settings. TikTok asks creators to label realistic AI content, and it is honest with your viewers.
3. Write a short caption (ideas below).
4. Add a few relevant hashtags, such as #airap or #rapduo, plus one for the occasion (#birthday, #bestfriend).
5. Tag the other person if they are on TikTok. Their repost is often where the views come from.

## Caption ideas

- "We've never rapped a day in our lives. Until now."
- "Me and my best friend after one coffee."
- "My grandma has bars. Who knew."
- "The duo nobody asked for."
- "Happy birthday to my day one 🎤"

## Tips for more views

- **Hook in the first second.** The caption on screen should tell people who the two are.
- **Post the reaction too.** Film the other person watching it for the first time and post it as a follow-up.
- **Make a series.** One duo per day for a week, with the same caption format, gives people a reason to follow.
- **Same video, three platforms.** The vertical MP4 works on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts without editing.

## Rules worth following

- Only use photos of people who agreed to be in the video.
- Children should only appear if you are their parent or legal guardian.
- Label it as AI-generated.
- The music and vocals come from a built-in rap performance; you cannot add your own song, and results vary between renders.

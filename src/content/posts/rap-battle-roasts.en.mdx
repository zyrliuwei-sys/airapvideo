**The short answer:** a good roast exaggerates a small, harmless habit until it is ridiculous, then lands the punchline on the last word. Roast habits, never insecurities. Here are 25 original, clean lines you can use on a friend, sorted by target.

## The rules first

- **Roast habits, not people.** Bad driving, yes. Looks, weight, money or family, no.
- **They should laugh first.** If you are not sure they would, cut the line.
- **Punch on the last word.** Set up in line one, rhyme the hit at the end of line two.
- **End with love.** The best battles finish with a hug, not a grudge.

## Their cooking

> You cooked us some pasta, I'm calling it brave,
> the noodles are crunchy, the sauce needs a grave.

> Your kitchen's a crime scene, the smoke alarm sings,
> it knows you so well it just waits in the wings.

> You burned a salad, I didn't know how,
> chef, you're a legend, take a bow now.

## Their driving

> You drive like the GPS owes you a fee,
> three wrong turns later and we're out at sea.

> Your parking's an art, it's abstract at best,
> two spaces, one car, and a cone on your chest.

> You signal at random, you merge like a dare,
> the other cars pray when they see you out there.

## Their phone

> Your phone's always dead, it's a fashion accessory,
> calling you up is a historical memory.

> You reply in three days with a single "lol",
> your texts move slower than snail mail at all.

> Your screen's so cracked it's a map of the town,
> you swipe it and bleed, but you won't put it down.

## Their music taste

> Your playlist's a mystery, a sad karaoke,
> one song on repeat since the year you were broke-y.

> You say you love rap but you skip every verse,
> you're only here for the hook, and it's getting worse.

> You turn up the bass in a two-door sedan,
> the windows are shaking, and so is your fan.

## Their style

> You dress like you lost a bet with the night,
> socks with your sandals, and both of them white.

> You bought a new jacket, it's louder than you,
> I can't hear your bars, cause the jacket's bars too.

## Their habits

> You're "five minutes out" for an hour or two,
> the clock's filed a missing report about you.

> You said you'd go running, you bought all the gear,
> the shoes are still boxed, and it's nearly a year.

> You nap like a champion, gold every round,
> you'd sleep through a concert if they turned it loud.

> You start every story with "this'll be quick",
> forty minutes later, my coffee's a brick.

> You borrowed my hoodie in 2019,
> it's living with you now, it's part of your scene.

## Closers that end on love

> I roast you this hard cause I know you can take it,
> best friend on the mic, and we both know we made it.

> All jokes aside, you're the best that I know,
> now give me my hoodie back before I go.

> You're late and you're loud and you cook like a storm,
> but you're my day one, so that's the norm.

> I came here to roast, I ran out of disses,
> happy birthday, legend, these are my wishes.

> We battle like rivals, but here's the plain truth:
> no one I'd rather be roasting since youth.

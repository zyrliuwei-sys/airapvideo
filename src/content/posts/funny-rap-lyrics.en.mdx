## Funny back-and-forth rap lyrics for two people

A duo rap gets funnier when the second line answers the first. One line each:

> **A:** I'm the brains of this duo, I planned the whole day,
> **B:** you planned it so well that we're lost on the way.

> **A:** I cooked us a dinner, a five-star affair,
> **B:** the smoke alarm clapped, so at least someone cared.

## How do you write a funny rap?

1. **Pick something small and universal.** Laundry beats politics.
2. **Rap it like it is epic.** The more serious the delivery, the funnier the subject.
3. **Land the joke on the last word.** Set it up in line one, rhyme the punchline at the end of line two.
4. **Add one real detail.** "Forty-two items and no bread" is funnier than "I bought too much".

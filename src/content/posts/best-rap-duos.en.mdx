**The short answer:** OutKast is the usual pick for the best rap duo of all time, with Eric B. & Rakim, Mobb Deep, Gang Starr, UGK, Clipse and Run the Jewels close behind. What they share is contrast: two different voices, or a rapper and a producer, that sound better together than either does alone.

The list below covers 15 duos across four decades. Each entry has a record to start with.

## Why each one made the list

**OutKast.** Two voices that could not be more different: André's left turns and Big Boi's precision. _Speakerboxxx/The Love Below_ won the Grammy for Album of the Year, still a rare feat for a rap record.

**Eric B. & Rakim.** Rakim's calm, internal-rhyme style changed what rapping could sound like. Most rappers who came after owe him something.

**Mobb Deep.** Prodigy's verses and Havoc's dark, minimal beats defined a whole mood of New York rap in the mid-90s.

**Gang Starr.** The template for the rapper-producer duo: Guru's steady voice over DJ Premier's chopped samples and scratched hooks.

**UGK.** The duo that put Texas on the map. Pimp C's production and Bun B's verses made Southern rap impossible to ignore.

**Run the Jewels.** Proof that a duo can start late. El-P and Killer Mike were both veterans when they teamed up, and the chemistry was instant.

**Clipse.** Two brothers, cold delivery, and Neptunes production that still sounds futuristic.

**EPMD.** Laid-back delivery and funk samples. Their name is short for "Erick and Parrish Making Dollars".

**Black Star.** Their 1998 debut is a conscious-rap landmark that still gets passed on; a second album, _No Fear of Time_, followed in 2022.

**Pete Rock & CL Smooth.** "They Reminisce Over You (T.R.O.Y.)" alone would earn them a place.

**Method Man & Redman.** The funniest pairing on the list, on record and on screen.

**DJ Jazzy Jeff & The Fresh Prince.** Won the first Grammy ever given for a rap performance, for "Parents Just Don't Understand".

**8Ball & MJG.** Memphis storytelling with a smooth, soulful sound.

**Kids See Ghosts.** A short album that hit hard, and a rare collaboration that sounds like a real group.

**Rae Sremmurd.** Two brothers who made "Black Beatles" one of the biggest songs of 2016.

## What makes a great rap duo

1. **Contrast.** Two styles that are clearly different: loud and calm, playful and serious.
2. **Chemistry.** Back-and-forth verses, finished sentences, shared hooks.
3. **One sound.** Whether it is a shared producer or a shared city, the best duos sound like a unit.

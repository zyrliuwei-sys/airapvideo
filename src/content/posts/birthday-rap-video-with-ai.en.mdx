A birthday message is easy to forget. A 10-second video of you and the birthday person rapping together on a stage is not. With an AI rap video generator you can make one from two photos, with no filming and no editing, and still have it ready before the cake is cut.

This guide walks through making a birthday rap video that feels personal, from picking the photos to sending it at the right moment.

## Why a rap video works as a birthday gift

- **It is about the two of you.** The video uses real photos of both people, so it reads as a personal message, not a template card.
- **It is short.** Ten seconds is long enough to make someone laugh and short enough to watch again.
- **It is made to share.** The clip is a vertical 9:16 MP4, which drops straight into a group chat, a story or a TikTok.

## Step 1: Choose the two photos

You need one photo of yourself and one of the birthday person. A few rules make a big difference:

- **One person per photo.** If your best photo of them is a group shot, crop it down to just them.
- **Face clearly visible.** Sharp, evenly lit, roughly facing the camera. Sunglasses and heavy shadows make the result less convincing.
- **Similar framing.** Two waist-up photos look like they belong on one stage. A tight selfie next to a full-body shot does not.

![A selfie works well as long as the face is clear and well lit](/imgs/blog/birthday-selfie.jpg)

A photo from a trip you took together is a nice touch. The outfits carry over into the video, so it brings back a shared memory without you having to explain it. For more detail, see [which photos work best for an AI rap video](/blog/best-photos-for-ai-rap-video).

## Step 2: Decide who leads

In the video, the person on the left leads the verse and the person on the right hypes. For a birthday, put the birthday person on the left. It is their day, so they get the spotlight.

## Step 4: Plan the surprise

The video lands better with a bit of timing:

- **Midnight message.** Send it right as the birthday starts. It will be the first thing they see.
- **At the party.** Cast it to a TV or pass a phone around. A rap video plays well in a room full of people.
- **In the group chat.** Post it where all your friends can see it, and the replies become part of the gift.

## Step 5: Write a short caption

The video is short, so the caption should be too. A few that work:

- "Happy birthday to the only person I'd share a mic with."
- "Ten years of friendship, ten seconds of bars. Happy birthday."
- "You asked for nothing this year. You got a rap video."

If you post it publicly, add a note that it was made with AI.

## A few things to keep in mind

- **Ask first if you post publicly.** Sending it privately to the birthday person is one thing. Posting their face on TikTok is another. Check they are happy with it.
- **Children.** Only include a child if you are their parent or legal guardian.
- **The music is built in.** The video uses a built-in rap performance with its own beat and vocals. You cannot upload a song or custom lyrics, and the audio and lip sync can vary between renders.

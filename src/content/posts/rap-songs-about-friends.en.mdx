## What is each friendship rap song about?

### "Friends" – Whodini (1984)

The original friendship rap. It asks a simple question, "friends, how many of us have them?", and it still works as a toast forty years later.

### "Just a Friend" – Biz Markie (1989)

The funniest song on the list. Biz falls for someone who keeps saying the guy on the phone is "just a friend". The off-key chorus is the whole point.

### "Life Goes On" – 2Pac (1996)

A song for friends who are gone, written as a celebration rather than a lament.

![Friends walking along the train tracks](/imgs/blog/friends-tracks.jpg)

### "I'll Be Missing You" – Puff Daddy & Faith Evans feat. 112 (1997)

Built on The Police's "Every Breath You Take", it became one of the best-known tribute songs in any genre.

### "Young, Wild & Free" – Snoop Dogg & Wiz Khalifa feat. Bruno Mars (2011)

The road-trip song. Play it when the whole group is in one car.

### "No New Friends" – DJ Khaled feat. Drake, Rick Ross & Lil Wayne (2013)

A loyalty anthem: keep the people who were there before things got good.

### "My Homies Still" – Lil Wayne feat. Big Sean (2013)

Same idea, lighter tone. Success changes a lot, but not the crew.

### "See You Again" – Wiz Khalifa feat. Charlie Puth (2015)

Written for _Furious 7_ as a tribute to Paul Walker, it became the defining modern song about losing a friend.

### "Real Friends" – Kanye West (2016)

The honest one: the friends and relatives you drift away from, and your own part in it.

![Friends sitting together above a valley](/imgs/blog/funny-laugh.jpg)

## Which friendship rap song fits which moment?

- **Birthday toast:** "Friends" or "Young, Wild & Free".
- **Road trip:** "Young, Wild & Free".
- **Group chat roast:** "Just a Friend".
- **Memorial or tribute:** "See You Again", "I'll Be Missing You" or "Life Goes On".
- **Reunion:** "My Homies Still".

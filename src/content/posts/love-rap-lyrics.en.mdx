**The short answer:** the best love rap lyrics are specific and a little funny. Mention one real thing about your partner, then land a sweet line on the last word. Below are 16 original bars you can use in a card, a caption or an anniversary post, plus six classic hip-hop love songs.

## Sweet

> You're the beat in my head when the room's too quiet,
> the reason I smile when I'm trying to hide it.

> I'd give you the moon but it's out of my range,
> so here's all my fries, and that's a big change.

> Out of every song on my all-time list,
> you're the one track I'd never skip.

> If love was a verse, then you'd be the hook,
> the part I come back to, the page in the book.

## Funny

> You steal all the blankets, you steal all the fries,
> but you stole my heart first, so I let it slide.

> You said "I'm not hungry" and then ate my plate,
> still you're my favorite, you're worth the wait.

> We argue for an hour on what we should eat,
> then end up at the same place, same table, same seat.

> You snore like a bass drum, you hog the whole bed,
> but I'd still pick you over sleep instead.

## For an anniversary

> One year, two years, I've lost count of the days,
> I'm still choosing you in a hundred small ways.

> From the first awkward date to the keys on the hook,
> we wrote our own story, and it's my favorite book.

> They said it won't last, now look at the score,
> another year down, I'm signing for more.

> Same couch, same jokes, same coffee at nine,

## For Valentine's Day

> Roses are red and my rhymes are on fire,
> you're the only one I'd ever hire.

> No chocolate, no flowers, I'm breaking the code,
> I wrote you a rap, now that's love, I'm told.

> I don't need a card with a poem inside,
> I've got you on the mic, and you're by my side.

> Cupid's a rapper, his arrow's a bar,
> it hit me the day that I saw who you are.

## How to write your own

1. **Start with one detail.** The way they laugh, a habit, your first date.
2. **Set it up, then land it.** Line one is the setup, the last word of line two is the payoff.
3. **Mix sweet and funny.** Pure sweetness can feel like a greeting card. A little teasing makes it yours.
